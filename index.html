Telco360 — Customer Churn Analytics

End-to-end telecom churn analysis → strategic retention framework

Impact & Results
  • Analyzed telecom customer data for 7,043 customers using Excel (Tracelight AI, Claude) + Julius AI + Manus AI, revealing a 26.54% overall churn rate and 5 critical risk segments.
  • Identified month-to-month contracts churning at 42.71%, first-year customers at 47.68%, and fiber optic users at 41.89%.
  • Discovered electronic check payers churn at 45.29% vs. only 2.83% for 2-year contract customers.
  • Delivered 4 data-backed retention recommendations + a live BI dashboard + executive Word report.

BevAnalytica — US Beverage Revenue Tracker

Power BI deep-dive into $8M annual cold drink sales across all 50 US states

Impact & Results
  • Built an interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing $8M in annual revenue and 16.45M units sold across all 50 US states using DAX, geospatial mapping & data modeling.
  • Uncovered a 2× summer revenue surge ($0.5M/mo in Q1 → $1.1M/mo at peak), enabling seasonal inventory planning for 6 major brands — Coca-Cola, Sprite, Fanta, Powerade, Diet Coke & Dasani.
  • Identified geographic revenue concentration: CA, TX & FL account for ~40% of total revenue — informing targeted distribution strategy.
  • Delivered 6 strategic recommendations on pricing ($0.48 avg/unit), seasonal optimization & geographic expansion + executive Word report.

British Airways — Customer Sentiment Analysis

Tableau-powered analysis of 2,000+ airline reviews across 6 years (2017–2023)

Impact & Results
  • Analyzed 2,000+ British Airways customer reviews (2017–2023) using Tableau, surfacing an average overall rating of 4.2/10 with critical service gaps.
